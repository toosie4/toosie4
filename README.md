## What I'm exploring

I'm learning neural networks from the inside out.

A working model is only the beginning. I want to know what it learned, why an approach worked, and what made it break.

I learn by experimenting, reading, and building small things myself.

My other field is physical networking.

I plan and maintain router-based networks for households, offices, and other shared spaces, from initial design and configuration to stubborn coverage problems and ongoing troubleshooting.

Reliability matters when people depend on the network every day.

## Current interests

- Training small neural networks
- Understanding what changes a model's behavior
- Designing and configuring router networks for real environments
- Troubleshooting the awkward reliability problems that appear when hardware, coverage, and people meet

## Let's build something

I'd like to meet people building in these areas.

A good collaboration has room to test ideas, learn from each other, and solve a problem that matters beyond a demo. It could involve neural networks, machine learning, network infrastructure, or an interesting mix of them.

That overlap is especially interesting to me.

If that sounds close to something you're working on, [start a conversation](https://github.com/toosie4/toosie4/issues/new?title=Collaboration%20idea).
